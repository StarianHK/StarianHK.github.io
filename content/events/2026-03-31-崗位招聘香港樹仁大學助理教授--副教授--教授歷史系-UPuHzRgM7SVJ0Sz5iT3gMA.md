崗位招聘

01

Description

Hong Kong Shue Yan University

Assistant/Associate/Full Professor of History

The Department of History is inviting applications for a full-time Assistant Professor or above to start in September 2026. Rank will be commensurate with qualifications, experience, and achievements. Candidates should have a doctorate degree in history or related fields and demonstrate the ability to effectively teach at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels while pursuing an active research agenda. The field of specialization is imperial China, history of Hong Kong, history of education, digital humanities, and/or world history (international relations). Preference will be given to those applicants who have at least two years of teaching experience and a good track record in research and publication. Proficiency in English is required.

02

About the University

Hong Kong Shue Yan University is the first private school in Hong Kong, with a student body of around 5,000 and a diversity of international academic staff, ranked by QS within the range of 300-350 and located on the top of Braemar Hill over the beautiful Victorian Bay. It aims to enable students to cultivate a whole, balanced personality and realize their full potential as individuals, as scholars, and as good citizens.

03

Application Instructions

Applications should be sent to the University Secretary(recruit_ap@hksyu.edu)with the following documents:

(1)a cover letter, (2)a complete CV including a list of publications, (3) two representative publications, (4)three references to be sent directly from the referees, and (5) an application form (https://www.hksyu.edu/assets/careers/Application%20Form%20for%20Academic%2 0Positions.doc).

For further information about the University and the Department of History, please visit www.hksyu.edu; for enquiries, please send emails to Ms. Anna Hui: history@hksyu.edu. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. Personal data received will be used for recruitment purposes only.

04

Closing Date

The process of the selection of candidates will start after 30 March 2026 and not end until the position is filled. Early submissions are particularly welcomed and will receive early consideration.
