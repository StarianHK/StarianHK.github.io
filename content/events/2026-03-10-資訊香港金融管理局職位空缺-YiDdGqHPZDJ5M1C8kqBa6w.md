香港金融管理局

職位空缺

香港金融管理局（金管局）是香港的中央銀行機構，成立於1993年4月1日，由外匯基金管理局與銀行業監理處合併而成。金管局的四項主要職能為：

在聯繫匯率制度的架構內維持貨幣穩定；

促進金融體系，包括銀行體系的穩定與健全；

協助鞏固香港的國際金融中心地位，包括維持與發展香港的金融基建；及

管理外匯基金。

職位空缺

01

Student Ambassador Programme

(AMLD)

Involvement of the Student Ambassadors

The student ambassadors will participate in this programme on a part-time basis and are expected to work on weekdays or weekends as needed.  Their working schedule will be communicated in advance, taking into account operational requirements and the timing of campaigns and events.  The student ambassadors are encouraged to work, on average, at least two half-day shifts or one whole-day shift per month.

Requirements

Non-final year local university students in academic year 2026-27 from any discipline

Fluency in Cantonese, English, and Putonghua, with basic proficiency to communicate in foreign languages (e.g. Indonesian, Filipino) an advantage

Strong communication and interpersonal skills

Previous experience in event planning and community outreaches an advantage

02

Manager (Hong Kong Institute for

Monetary and Financial Research)

Key Responsibilities

Conduct rigorous and policy-relevant research on topics of interest to the AoF and the HKIMR, and report main research findings to both academic and non-academic audiences

Contribute to the administration of the HKIMR, such as preparing meeting materials and liaising with the Institute’s main stakeholders (e.g. Advisers, Directors, Visiting Scholars, etc.)

Communicate with multiple parties with cross-discipline, and diverse research and technical backgrounds

Requirements

Postgraduate degree in one or more of the following broad fields of specialisations: economics, finance, applied econometrics. A PhD degree (obtained or close to completion) and interest in empirical macroeconomics or finance is a definite advantage

At least 3 years of experience in conducting academic research / thought leadership at central banks / universities / research institutes / international institutions

Good analytical, programming and synthesis skills

Good command of English, good drafting and presentation skills

(Selected candidates will be seconded from the HKMA to work in the HKIMR.)

Closing Date(s): 28 March 2026

03

Ph.D. Fellowship Programme

Key Responsibilities

Conduct independent research: advance dissertation research with the aim of producing a high-quality working paper

Deliver a research seminar: present research to department staff toward the end of the fellowship programme

Contribute to departmental work: participate in research discussions, and perform other projects as assigned

Requirements

Ph.D. candidates in economics, finance or related fields

Completed field exams and are in the dissertation stage of Ph.D., preferably in final year

Excellent quantitative skills with experience in conducting research in relevant fields

Good interpersonal skills and interest in conducting policy-relevant research

Good command of both written and spoken English and Chinese

(Please include 1-2 letters of recommendation from academic advisor(s) and a research proposal that is no more than 5 pages together with your application.)

Closing Date(s): 28 March 2026

04

Director, Solutions Delivery

(Tax Services)

Key Responsibilities

Report to the Executive Director (ED) of Solution Delivery, support the Global Securities System (GSS) project, and serve as a subject matter expert for tax services and solutions

Coordinate with internal and external stakeholders on system selection and delivery, serving as a key liaison between operations and IT teams to ensure smooth roll-out of new systems

Work with the GSS project manager on end-to-end project management, including maintaining project timetables, monitoring key deliverables and project budgets, and producing progress reports

Work with key stakeholders to develop the operating models for GSS asset servicing

Requirements

University degree

At least 8 years of relevant experience in the securities services industry, preferably acquired from market infrastructure providers or custodian banks

Extensive experience in implementing tax servicing systems and solutions, including:

🔸Withholding tax management

🔸Tax reclamation

🔸Regulatory compliance, assisting with tax documentation, such as W-8/W-9 forms, and compliance with regulations like FATCA and Qualified Intermediary (QI) agreements

Experience in managing global tax servicing teams in a global asset servicing operations hub

Experience in managing a sizeable team and a track record of delivering complex projects

Familiarity with financial markets, preferably in both fixed income and equity products

Well organised, detail-oriented with effective communication skills

Strong problem-solving and analytical skills, and highly motivated with the ability to manage competing priorities and meet deadlines

Excellent command of both spoken and written English and Chinese

Candidates with supplementary experience and qualifications will also be considered, even if they do not meet all of the specified requirements above

(Candidates with less experience may be considered for an offer of appointment at Associate level.)

Closing Date(s): 21 March 2026

05

Senior Associate

(Digital Asset Platform)

Key Responsibilities

Participate in the development, programming, testing and deployment of Digital Asset Platform to support the entire life cycle of debt securities on blockchains

Audit, optimize, and document smart contracts to ensure security, gas efficiency, and maintainability

Participate in code reviews, security assessments, and troubleshooting of blockchain-related issues

Maintain decentralized applications (dApps) and related backend services

Keep abreast of blockchain trends, best practices, and emerging technologies

Requirements

University degree in computer science, information technology, engineering or related fields

At least 3 years of relevant experience in a well-established financial organisation or leading financial institutions, in blockchain development with hands-on smart contract programming

Good understanding of blockchain protocols and standards including consensus mechanisms, token standards (ERC-20), DeFi architectures, high availability architecture and DAO infrastructures

Good knowledge of cryptographic principles, security best practices, and smart contract vulnerabilities

Experience in deployment of solution on public / private cloud

Experience in using version control (Git) and agile development methodologies

Proficiency in Solidity, Java, Go, or other blockchain-oriented languages

Knowledge of using SWIFT in Custody Services is a plus

Self-driven and a good team player

Good analytical, interpersonal and communication skills

Candidates with supplementary experience and qualifications will also be considered, even if they do not meet all of the specified requirements above

(Candidates with less experience may be considered for an offer of appointment at Associate level.)

Closing Date(s): 21 March 2026

06

Associate Portfolio Manager

(Private Markets)

Key Responsibilities

Support the operations and investment activities of the Long-Term Growth Portfolio (“LTGP”) focusing on private equity and co-investments

Conduct due diligence, build financial models, analyse market dynamics and monitor investment trends to support deal sourcing, preparation of investment memorandum and execution for co-investments, direct investments and private equity funds

Source, maintain and deepen investment relationship with counterparties

Monitor global, regional, and local economic and financial market developments

Perform all tasks according to internal guidelines and international best practices

Requirements

University degree with good academic results in business, finance, economics or related fields

At least 3 years of relevant experience with strong financial modeling capability, preferably in private equity, investment banking, or management consulting. Candidate should be able to build an LBO model

Co-investment experience in private equity a plus

General knowledge of various private market asset classes

High versatility and flexibility, and attention to detail

Good logical and critical thinking with a sharp and analytical mind; good presentation, communication, and writing skills

Good command of both English and Chinese; proficiency in Putonghua an advantage

A team player with good interpersonal skills

Closing Date(s): 14 March 2026

07

Senior Associate

(Application System Development)

Key Responsibilities

Responsible for the development as well as maintenance of enterprise application systems.

Participate in full SDLC activities including system analysis, design, coding, testing, deployment and ongoing support

Work with internal and external parties to develop and implement solutions and deliver business outcomes

Set up and maintain testing environments to support testing activities

Maintain effective communication with stakeholders and supervisors by proactively providing regular updates on project status, risks and issues

Keep abreast of relevant technological advancements, development of industry standards and best practices. Actively review the CMU’s existing systems and identify areas for continuous improvement

Requirements

University degree in information technology, computer science or related fields

At least 3 years of relevant working experience in a well-established organization, including development and support of information systems

Solid experience in Java, JMS, JPA, Spring framework, Oracle database, Hibernate, Message-driven EJB, JSON, Open API, Vue.js, IBM WebSphere and MQ

Good knowledge of Agile methodology and DevSecOps

Knowledge of Central Securities Depositories (CMU, Euroclear, Clearstream) and SWIFT MT/MX would be an advantage

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to make sound decisions

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to effectively communicate complex information technology concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders

Candidates with less experience and qualifications will also be considered, even if they do not meet all of the specified requirements above

(Candidates with less experience may be considered for an offer of appointment at Associate level.)

Closing Date(s): 14 March 2026
